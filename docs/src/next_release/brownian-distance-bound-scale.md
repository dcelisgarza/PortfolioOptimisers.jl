# [The model of `BrownianDistanceVariance`](@id migration-next-brownian-distance-bound-scale)

- **The default formulation changed.** `BrownianDistanceVariance()` now takes `alg2 = SortingNetworkOrderedWeightsArray()`. Before this change it took `alg2 = NormOneConeBrownianDistanceVariance()`. The default of `alg1` stays `QuadRiskExpr()`. The old default wrote the square of the sum of the distances into the objective, so its quadratic matrix was dense in the ``T^2`` distances. On 5 assets it took 33 s at ``T = 50``, and at ``T = 100`` the process ran out of memory at 5.4 GB. The new default solves ``T = 300`` in less than 8 s, and ``T = 600`` on 20 assets in about one minute. To keep the old pair rows, pass `alg2 = IneqBrownianDistanceVariance()` or `alg2 = NormOneConeBrownianDistanceVariance()`.
- **The model states the closed form ``2 \sigma^2 + \bar{D}^2``.** The variance term reads the centred returns, so it needs no distance variable. Only the mean distance needs rows, and `alg2` now selects them. `alg2` also takes `ExactOrderedWeightsArray()` and `SortingNetworkOrderedWeightsArray()`, because the mean distance is an ordered weighted average of the returns. `alg1` also takes `SquaredSOCRiskExpr()`. The functor and the optimum do not change. The weights of a solve move at the tolerance of the solver: by up to `9e-5` relative on 20 assets at the default tolerances of Clarabel, where the old and the new weights are both within `3e-4` of a solve at `1e-11`.
- **The registered keys of the model changed.** `Dt` is now a vector of the ``T (T - 1) / 2`` distances of the pairs ``i > j``, and only the two pair formulations register it and `Dx`. Before this change the model registered `Dt` as a symmetric ``T \times T`` matrix. The model now registers `bdvariance_md`, the mean distance, `bdvariance_md_ub`, the expression of the rows that bounds it, `bdvariance_mu`, the mean return, and `bdvariance_xc`, the scaled centred returns. Under `RSOCRiskExpr`, `tDt`, `tsum_Dt`, `rsoc_Dt` and `rsoc_sum_Dt` are now `t_bdvariance_var`, `t_bdvariance_md`, `rsoc_bdvariance_var` and `rsoc_bdvariance_md`, and they hold ``2 \sigma^2`` and ``\bar{D}^2``.
- **`RiskBudgeting` with a bound on the measure** stops Clarabel at `SLOW_PROGRESS` with the default settings of Clarabel, before this change and after it. The docstring of `BrownianDistanceVariance` states the measurement and the setting `"min_switch_step_length" => 1e-3`, with which every measured case solves.

## [If you extend the library](@id migration-next-brownian-distance-bound-scale-extending)

- `set_brownian_distance_variance_constraints!` is now `set_brownian_distance_mean!`. It takes the asset returns, the net returns and ``T``, and it returns the expression of the mean distance. `set_brownian_distance_risk_constraint!` takes the scaled centred returns and the mean distance in place of the distance matrix and ``1 / T^2``. `set_brownian_distance_bounds_expr!` takes no ``T``.

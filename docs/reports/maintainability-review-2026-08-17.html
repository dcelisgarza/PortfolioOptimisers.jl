Maintainability review — PortfolioOptimisers.jl

2026-08-17 · branch dev

This pass looked for one shape re-encoded across many sites, and for copies that have already drifted apart. Six areas were swept: risk measures, the optimisation layer, moments and preprocessing, prior and uncertainty sets, constraint generation and phylogeny, and the plotting extension. Every count below was verified against the working tree. Nine of the twelve candidates carry a defect that was reproduced by running it, not merely read.

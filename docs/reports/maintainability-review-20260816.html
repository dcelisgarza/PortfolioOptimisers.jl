Problem. @propagatable stamps out 231 methods from 143 struct declarations, none of which grep or jump-to-definition can find. It also encodes a reflection contract: it emits StructName(; field = …) for every field, so every field name must also be a keyword of the outer constructor, and an @pprop-tagged field name must additionally exist as a property of the Prior Result. Direction. Keep the codegen; add the definition-time check @windowed_estimator already performs (08_Moments/01_Base_Moments.jl:1355), so the contract is enforced at the declaration rather than at the first call.

Top recommendation — close the six silent drifts, then delete the forwarders that breed them

Six candidates on this page are copies that have already diverged into a wrong number with no diagnostic: the gross budget (10 of 11 sites), the unweighted var, the exported drawdowns, the dropped fees, the unclamped Gerber2 and the missing zero guard. Each is a one-line correction, so take them first — they are latent wrong answers, not tidy-ups, and the gross budget is the one to start with, because it changes the feasible set of four optimisers while reporting success. Then take candidate 9 as the structural follow-up: 18 hand-written forwarders over a generic that already exists, which have settled into five different contracts. It is the largest single collapse on the page, and it removes the mechanism — retyping a shape per site — that produced most of the drift above.

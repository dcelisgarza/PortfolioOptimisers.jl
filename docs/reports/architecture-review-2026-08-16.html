The house pattern already exists.

@fprop (160 uses), @vprop (153), @propagatable (120), @pprop/@cprop/@wprop (63), and @windowed_estimator (ADR 0039) all express the same shape: one declaration per type behind a deep module that reads it. A · Give the optimiser head an interface for the settings it carries

src/20_Optimisation/11_MeanRisk.jl:700-709 · 12_FactorRiskContribution.jl:321-335

13_NearOptimalCentering.jl:1126-1144, 1181-1196 · 14_RiskBudgeting.jl:633-742 · 15_RelaxedRiskBudgeting.jl:448-484

09_JuMPConstraints/04_WeightConstraints.jl:110-149 · test/test_03b_jump_model_assembly.jl:19-38

Before · the head is six copies

After · one declaration carries the settings

Problem

The head carries two unrelated jobs — how the weight variables are shaped, which genuinely varies, and which JuMPOptimiser settings reach the model, which does not. The second job has no interface, so it is six hand-maintained copies, and they have drifted.

Solution

One declaration that carries every budget and scale field into the model, called once per head. Keep the weight shaping where it is. Add the head to the seam lock's remit so the next field cannot fall through.

Wins

  • locality: one place per new field
  • leverage: one declaration, five heads
  • Fixes a live silent drop
  • Seam lock can state the rule
  • Catches the stale test call
  • Interface stops naming ten lists
Touches ADR 0008 §3 — which keeps the head per-optimiser. This does not overturn it. The finding is that §3 conflated two jobs; only one of them varies.
Two further drifts in the same head

k is established three ways. get_k's error names one producer (set_maximum_ratio_factor_variables!), but only MeanRisk and Factor Risk Contribution call it; Near Optimal Centering and Relaxed Risk Budgeting write a raw expression, and Risk Budgeting declares a raw variable.

test_03b_jump_model_assembly.jl:24 calls set_maximum_ratio_factor_variables! with three arguments against a two-argument signature. A variadic fallback absorbs it, so the solver-free test cannot exercise the MaximumRatio branch and never says so.

Unconstrained Near Optimal Centering hand-writes all 20 ProcessedJuMPOptimiserAttributes fields (13_NearOptimalCentering.jl:1149-1170) — the reverse of the remap that jump_optimiser_from_attributes derives by reflection, and the same class of hand list whose past scard omission that function's own comment records.

After · both read one declaration

Problem

Factory gets container recursion for free from @fprop. The JuMP entry point does not: each container must hand-write a forwarding method, and two did not. The interface is 15 resolver methods plus 15 slot declarations that nothing ties together — nearly as complex as the rule it encodes.

Solution

Have resolve_deferred_quantities consult deferred_slots, which already says a container "names its children and each child names its own slots". Forwarding becomes derived rather than written.

Wins

  • locality: one declaration per container
  • Deletes four forwarding methods
  • Closes two live gaps
  • Resolution testable without a solver
  • A missed container fails loudly
  • leverage: one rule, three entry points
The divergence is visible inside one function. 18_TrackingRiskMeasureConstraints.jl:465 computes the benchmark scalar through factory(ri, pr, …) — resolved — while :471 builds the model expression from the unresolved ri. Two quantities, one function, two resolution rules.
Extends ADR 0051 — which states that every type declaring resolve_deferred_quantities declares deferred_slots beside it. The counts are 15 and 15 across src/, and the two containers above declare neither. test/test_09e_deferred_quantity.jl is 1393 lines and covers neither.

C · Give the prior-fallback half of the resolution rule the seam the deferred half has

src/21_ExpectedReturns.jl:171, 235, 283, 333, 605-611, 660-664

src/19_RiskMeasures/27_ExpectedRisk.jl:211-219, 317-335 · 01_Base_RiskMeasures.jl:1629-1634

Before · the rule written twice per measure

After · one rule, one place

Problem

ADR 0051 factored the deferred half of the precedence rule into a seam and deliberately left the fallback half where it was. The fallback is now re-encoded once per builder and once per @pprop tag, with no single place that states it — and the fourth consumer to need it does not apply it.

Solution

Make both halves reachable through the same per-type declaration, so a consumer holding a Prior applies the whole rule by naming one function. Route the expected_ratio family through it.

Wins

  • locality: the rule has one home
  • Fixes the ratio family's risk axis
  • Two selectors become one
  • Removes a documented test workaround
  • leverage: one rule, four consumers
  • Fixes the singular/vector asymmetry
Re-opens one clause of ADR 0051 — "the prior fallback for an unstated slot stays where it already was." Worth revisiting because a fourth consumer has appeared since, and the workaround is already written into a test comment at test/test_18r_value_level_multiplicity.jl:153-155 rather than fixed.
The same asymmetry inside one type

expected_risk(r::PrRM, w::VecVecNum, pr, …) calls factory(r, pr) first (21_ExpectedReturns.jl:662); the singular method at :605-611 does not. So expected_risk(errr, [w], pr)[1] works where expected_risk(errr, w, pr) throws.

The second full derivation that the two-argument entry point exists to prevent happens anyway, inside the very function that avoided it.

After · build once, pass the graph

Problem

Every Separation kernel takes (sep, nte, X) — a producer plus the data — although its behaviour depends only on the graph. The kernel re-runs the whole production chain, and a test or an extension holding a graph has no way in.

Solution

Give the kernels a graph-taking form beside the estimator-taking one — the same two-entry-point shape calc_weighted_adjacency_graph already has, for the reason its own docstring already gives. The estimator form becomes the wrapper.

Wins

  • Removes a redundant rebuild in four consumers
  • Deletes three test doubles
  • The interface becomes the test surface
  • The unreachable branch becomes testable
  • leverage: extensions get a real seam
  • locality: one build site, not four
The cost is already documented. calc_weighted_adjacency_graph's docstring (06_Phylogeny.jl:1620) prices the derivation at 98% of clusterise's runtime under VariationInfoDistance. phylogeny_matrix and phylogeny_features perform two full builds per call whenever the budget is a rule.

E · Put the unreachable-pair sentinel on the Separation family

src/11_Phylogeny/06_Phylogeny.jl:1886-1893, 1933-1939, 2403-2411 · src/13_Prior/13_FeaturePrior.jl:255-264

src/11_Phylogeny/01_Base_Phylogeny.jl:90-96 (the family's stated extension contract)

Before · one rule, four spellings

After · the family owns the rule

Problem

"Compare against the budget first, and short-circuit" is a required ordering constraint that lives in prose in three docstrings and is hand-written at four sites in four spellings. Two of them disagree. An invariant nothing can call is not part of an interface.

Solution

One predicate — or one "apply the budget" kernel — on the Separation family, which the four consumers call. The family already states its extension contract as "a struct and one method of each"; this is the third method.

Wins

  • Corrects the drifted copy
  • locality: one sentinel rule
  • The invariant becomes callable
  • Extensions inherit it
  • Both sentinels handled once
  • leverage: one rule, four consumers
Both sentinels punish an unguarded read, for opposite reasons. ReciprocalDecay overflows 1 + d at typemax(Int), which a fractional power turns into a DomainError. LinearDecay at Inf returns -Inf, which sorts every unreachable pair below every reachable one and raises nothing.

F · Give the factor lift a module of its own

src/13_Prior/03_FactorPrior.jl:188-229 · 08_FactorBlackLittermanPrior.jl:306-382

Before · one algorithm, three sites, no owner

After · the lift is the module

Problem

"Project a factor-axis Prior onto the asset axis through regression loadings, optionally add a residual block, and build the carrier" is one algorithm at three sites. Wins

  • locality: the lift has one home

  • The kwarg drift becomes one decision
  • The high-order lift gets the same seam

A drifted copy, in kwarg threading. 03_FactorPrior.jl:194 calls prior(pe.pe, F) and drops everything including strict; 08:324 and 09:394 both pass strict = strict. FactorPrior.pe admits BlackLittermanPrior and EntropyPoolingPrior, both of which resolve view names and honour strict.
Extends ADR 0046, which correctly records that these three sites do not fit forward_prior. This gives them the helper they do fit. Nothing is re-litigated.

Problem

H · Route the Range risk measures through the delegation seam they already have

src/20_Optimisation/20_RiskMeasureConstraints/21_GenericValueatRiskRangeConstraints.jl:30-41 (delegates, 12 lines)

…/07_*:106-168, 324-495 · 08_*:124-207 · 09_*:153-291 · 19_*:140-236 · 10_*:296-429 · 06_*:136-213

Before · the mass of the duplicating members

After · two calls and a sum

Problem

A range measure is the base measure applied twice — once to the losses, once to the gains with beta in place of alpha. The loss seam that expresses this is threaded through 11 builder signatures and has two working adapters. Seven of nine members ignore it.

Solution

Do what GenericValueatRiskRange does: hold or derive the two side measures and delegate twice. The alpha/beta split becomes a construction concern of the range type, not a builder concern. Leave the two fused formulations alone.

Wins

  • Deletes ~770 duplicated lines
  • locality: a base fix reaches its range
  • The seam gains seven adapters
  • leverage: one builder, two tails
  • A new range measure costs 12 lines
  • Interface stops naming _l / _h pairs
The mechanism is already there. 07_ConditionalXatRiskConstraints.jl:57-59 reads net_X = set_net_portfolio_returns!(…); if !loss; net_X = -net_X; end — which is precisely what every _h block re-derives by hand.

I · Bring index-suffixed Model State keys inside the accessor interface

src/20_Optimisation/08_Base_JuMPOptimisation.jl:1144-1266 (the policed mechanism)

Problem

ADR 0037 closed the hole for prefix-namespaced keys and inverted the lint's polarity. A second, older convention — the index suffix — carries the same do-not-collide invariant, is spelled by hand at 200 sites, and matches neither lock rule.

Solution

One operation taking (prefix, name, i) that resolves the composed key, so both axes are disambiguated in one place. Then the third lock rule can be written, and the tracking path stops carrying the same fact through two parameters.

Wins

  • Namespacing testable as a property
  • leverage: one operation, 200 sites
  • Closes ADR 0037's remaining half
Extends ADR 0037, using the ADR's own reasoning. It argued the polarity case decisively and then applied it to one of two mechanisms. The scope is what is incomplete, not the argument.

Genuinely deep, and worth protecting

Several things I expected to be friction turned out to be well-shaped. The report is more useful with them named, and three of them are the templates the candidates above should copy.

assemble_jump_model!

Model Assembly's 17-call sequence exists in exactly one place. Five optimisers call it once each. ADR 0008 landed and held — no drift in the middle.

risk_input_kind

~50 one-line declarations collapse what would be 50 expected_risk methods into three. Small interface, large behaviour, three real adapters. The pattern the other candidates should imitate.

AbstractSeparationDecayAlgorithm

One three-argument kernel, four members, and a contract enforced by a probing fallback the shipped members opt out of. The budget is an argument, not a field, so the two knobs cannot desync.

forward_prior · o_X · original_X

One storage field carrying the nothing, one always-a-matrix read, and a derived field list. No consumer can forget the fallback. Candidate G says it is under-applied, not wrong.

jump_optimiser_from_attributes

Derives an 18-field remap by reflection with a six-entry rename table, and carries the comment recording the hand-written version that once dropped scard.

run_step · Base.split

The Pipeline carries ~18 adapters behind one interface; Cross-Validation carries six schemes behind another. Both are one-method seams doing real work.

supported_risk_measures

Derives the compatibility table from the same supertypes the builders dispatch on, and the meta-optimisers intersect their children. No table to drift.

28_RiskMeasureTools.jl

189 lines carrying the settings-rewriting family for every measure through Accessors.@set. Zero per-type boilerplate.

the Degeneracy Guard's two homes

Clean, not leaky. ADR 0054's argument for the split holds up, and the predicate documents its own known over-reach rather than hiding it. No friction worth filing.

One number worth correcting: 06_Phylogeny.jl is 2810 lines, but 2182 of them are docstrings and only 627 are code. It is not a logic monolith. Its friction is a 58-name public surface on one page, which is what candidates D and E address.

Top recommendation

A · Give the optimiser head an interface for the settings it carries

It is the only candidate with a live, user-visible wrong answer. A caller who writes JuMPOptimiser(; gbgt = 2.0) and hands it to Risk Budgeting, Relaxed Risk Budgeting, Factor Risk Contribution or Near Optimal Centering gets a portfolio with no leverage cap and no diagnostic. The value is validated at construction, documented at length in field_dict and in CONTEXT §4, and then dropped.

It also has the best ratio of blast radius to payoff. The fix is one declaration and six call sites, it does not touch Model Assembly, and it makes two further drifts in the same head — the three ways k is established, and the stale three-argument call the solver-free test is silently absorbing — visible instead of latent.

If you would rather start somewhere with no behaviour change at all, take H · the Range risk measures — roughly 770 lines deleted through a seam that already has two working adapters, with ADR 0037's model-print oracle available to prove the output is unchanged.

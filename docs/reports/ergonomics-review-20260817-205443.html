Top recommendation — extend the slot guard to the default, then fix the assertion helpers

Start with card 1. It is the largest single block of caller friction on the surface — 35 of 92 risk measures answer the documented matrix entry point with a MethodError naming Nothing — and it is the cheapest to close, because the message a caller should see is already written and already fires for the neighbouring case: put a Covariance() in Variance.sigma and the library explains the slot, the reason and two remedies; leave the slot at its default and the same call reaches the kernel. One guard learns one more case. Then take card 2, where sixteen sites in the shared assertion helpers reach roughly 178 construction sites, and three of them currently tell the caller that the condition which just failed holds. Together the two touch no signature and no default, so they can land ahead of every naming question in this report.

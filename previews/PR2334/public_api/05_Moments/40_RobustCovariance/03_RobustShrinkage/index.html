abstract type AbstractRobustShrinkageIntensity <: AbstractAlgorithm

Abstract supertype for the rules that find the shrinkage intensity of a robust scatter from the data.

A shrunk robust scatter is a blend of the scatter of the data and of a target. The intensity is the weight of the target, in [0, 1]. A member of this family finds it when the fit runs. The slot also takes a number, the intensity itself.

struct ConditionNumberIntensity{__T_max_cond} <: AbstractSubsetShrinkageIntensity

Takes the smallest shrinkage intensity at which the condition number of the scatter is not above a bound.

Section 2.4 of [112] gives the rule for the shrunk minimum covariance determinant. The condition number is the one of the scatter in the coordinates that turn the target into the identity matrix. A scatter that meets the bound gets no shrinkage. For a subset the rule has a closed form. This library extends the rule to TylerShrinkage, where a bisection over the fits of the shape finds the intensity, at the pilot centre of the fit.

Mathematical definition

\[\begin{align} \rho &= \frac{a}{\kappa - 1 + a}\,, & a &= \max\left(0, \lambda_{\max} - \kappa \lambda_{\min}\right)\,. \end{align}\]

Where:

  • $\rho$: The shrinkage intensity.
  • $a$: The amount by which the scatter exceeds the bound.
  • $\kappa$: The bound of the condition number, max_cond.
  • $\lambda_{\min}$, $\lambda_{\max}$: The smallest and the largest eigenvalue of the scatter of the subset.

Fields

  • max_cond: Bound of the condition number of the scatter, max_cond > 1.

struct SphericityIntensity <: AbstractSubsetShrinkageIntensity

Takes the shrinkage intensity of the smallest mean squared error, from a robust estimate of the sphericity of the scatter.

The sphericity $\gamma$ is the mean of the squares of the eigenvalues over the square of their mean. It is one when the scatter is a multiple of the target. [113] estimates it from the spatial signs of the data, so an outlier has the influence of one direction and not of its size. This library takes the signs about the coordinatewise median, and that work takes them about the spatial median.

For MCDShrinkage the intensity is the optimum at the normal model, with the count of the subset. The signs come from every observation, because a subset of the nearest observations is more spherical than the data. For TylerShrinkage it is the optimum of [114] for the shape of Tyler's M-estimator, which holds over the elliptical distributions.

Mathematical definition

\[\begin{align} \hat{\gamma} &= \frac{n}{n - 1} \left(p \, \mathrm{tr}(\mathbf{S}^2) - \frac{p}{n}\right)\,, \\ \rho_{\mathrm{MCD}} &= \frac{(\hat{\gamma} + p)/(h - 1)}{\hat{\gamma} - 1 + (\hat{\gamma} + p)/(h - 1)}\,, \\ \rho_{\mathrm{Tyler}} &= 1 - \frac{n (\hat{\gamma} - 1)}{n (\hat{\gamma} - 1)(1 - 1/n) + (2\hat{\gamma} + p)(p - 1)/(p + 2)}\,. \end{align}\]

Where:

  • $\mathbf{S}$: The spatial sign covariance, the mean of the product moments of the unit vectors of the observations.
  • $\hat{\gamma}$: The sphericity, clamped into $[1, p]$.
  • $n$: The number of observations, or the effective count of the weights.
  • $h$: The number of observations of the subset. A fixed centre gives $h$ in place of $h - 1$.
  • $p$: The number of assets.

struct OracleIntensity <: AbstractRobustShrinkageIntensity

Takes the oracle intensity of the work that gives the form of the shrunk Tyler shape, with an estimate in place of the unknown scatter.

Each form of TylerShrinkage has its own oracle, the intensity of the smallest mean squared error when the weights of the fixed point read the true scatter.

  • TraceNormalisedShrinkage: Equation 13 of [115], with the normalised sample covariance of its Equation 14.
  • PenalisedShrinkage: Theorem 4 of [116] in the real case, with the Tyler shape when the sample gives one. Otherwise, the pilot is the penalised shape at the middle of the interval of the intensities at which it exists. That work leaves the choice of that pilot open.

Mathematical definition

\[\begin{align} \rho_{\mathrm{trace}} &= \frac{p^2 + (1 - 2/p) \, \mathrm{tr}(\mathbf{R}^2)}{p^2 - np - 2n + (n + 1 + 2(n - 1)/p) \, \mathrm{tr}(\mathbf{R}^2)}\,, \\ \rho_{\mathrm{penalised}} &= \frac{p - 2 + p \, \mathrm{tr}(\mathbf{V})}{p - 2 + p \, \mathrm{tr}(\mathbf{V}) + n (p + 2) (\mathrm{tr}(\mathbf{V}^{-2})/p - 1)}\,. \end{align}\]

Where:

  • $\mathbf{R}$: $p$ times the spatial sign covariance of the data.
  • $\mathbf{V}$: The pilot shape, with $\mathrm{tr}(\mathbf{V}^{-1}) = p$.
  • $n$: The number of observations, or the effective count of the weights.
  • $p$: The number of assets.

The rule clamps the intensity into $[0, 1]$.

struct AsymptoticIntensity <: AbstractRobustShrinkageIntensity

Takes the intensity of the smallest mean squared error when the numbers of observations and of assets grow together.

Proposition 2 of [117] gives an equation in the intensity for each form of TylerShrinkage, and shows that both forms then reach the same error. The rule finds the smallest intensity that reaches the value of the equation by a bisection over the fits of the shape, at the pilot centre of the fit.

Mathematical definition

\[\begin{align} \frac{\rho \, a(\rho)}{1 - \rho + \rho \, a(\rho)} &= r\,, & a(\rho) &= \frac{1}{n} \sum_{t=1}^{n} \frac{\boldsymbol{x}_t^\intercal \mathbf{V}(\rho)^{-1} \boldsymbol{x}_t}{\lVert \boldsymbol{x}_t \rVert^2}\,, \\ \frac{\rho}{\mathrm{tr}(\mathbf{V}(\rho))/p} &= r\,, & r &= \frac{p/n}{\mathrm{tr}(\mathbf{R}^2)/p - 1}\,. \end{align}\]

Where:

  • $\mathbf{V}(\rho)$: The shape at the intensity $\rho$. The first equation holds for TraceNormalisedShrinkage, and the second for PenalisedShrinkage.
  • $\mathbf{R}$: $p$ times the spatial sign covariance of the data.
  • $n$: The number of observations, or the effective count of the weights.
  • $p$: The number of assets.

A value $r$ of one or more gives the intensity one.

struct TraceNormalisedShrinkage <: AbstractTylerShrinkageAlgorithm

Blends each step of the shape equation with the target, and scales the blend to a fixed trace.

[115] shows that the iteration has one limit for each intensity in (0, 1), for any number of observations.

Where:

  • $\mathbf{V}$: The shape, in the coordinates that turn the target into the identity matrix.
  • $\rho$: The shrinkage intensity.
  • $\boldsymbol{x}_t$: The centred observation $t$, in the same coordinates.
  • $n$: The number of observations.

struct MCDShrinkage{__T_tgt, __T_intensity} <: AbstractEstimator

Shrinks the scatter of each subset of an MCDCovariance to a target, so the fit exists with fewer observations than assets.

MCDCovariance holds it in the field shr. The fit is then the minimum regularised covariance determinant of [112]: the subset whose shrunk scatter has the smallest determinant. The target does not change in the search.

The target tgt is a covariance matrix in the units of the data. A rule of CovarianceShrinkageTarget reads a robust pilot: the robust variances of the assets on the diagonal, and the sine transform of the Kendall correlations off it. The default DiagonalTarget is the diagonal of the robust variances, the identity target of [112] on its standardised data. ConstantCorrelationTarget is its equicorrelation target.

From the same starts and the same target, the fit agrees with the function CovMrcd of the R package rrcov, version 1.7-7. Two parts differ. That package takes another rank correlation for its equicorrelation target, see mcd_pilot. With fewer observations than assets, its starts read an arbitrary basis of the null space of each correlation estimate, and the starts of this library do not, see regular_directions.

Fields

  • tgt: Shrinkage target. A rule of CovarianceShrinkageTarget, or a fixed positive definite matrix in the units of the data.
  • intensity: Shrinkage intensity, the weight of the target. A number in [0, 1], or a rule that finds it from the data.

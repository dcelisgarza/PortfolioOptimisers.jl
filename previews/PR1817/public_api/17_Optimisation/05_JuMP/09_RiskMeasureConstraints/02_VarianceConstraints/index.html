variance_risk_bounds_val(
    bound::FrontierBoundEstimator,
    ub::Frontier
) -> Frontier{<:Integer, Int64, <:FrontierBoundEstimator}

Convert a bound into the units of the expression that it acts on.

A caller states the bound of a second moment in the units of the measure. A formulation whose bound expression is the square root takes the square root of the bound, and a formulation whose bound expression is the square takes its square.

  • LinearBound: The bound, unchanged.
  • SquareRootBound: The square root of the bound, entry by entry for a vector.
  • SquaredBound: The square of the bound, entry by entry for a vector.

A Frontier keeps its N, and takes bound as its FrontierBoundEstimator with a factor of 1. The frontier solve converts the ends of its span when it builds the sweep. nothing gives nothing.

Arguments

  • bound::FrontierBoundEstimator: The conversion.
  • ub: The bound, a number, a vector, a Frontier or nothing.

Returns

  • The converted bound, or nothing when ub is nothing.

Add a variance to model, bound it by settings.ub, and add it to the risk that the objective reads.

The bound settings.ub is in the units of a variance. variance_risk_bounds_expr names the expression of degree one that it acts on, and variance_risk_bounds_val converts it to the units of that expression.

When the variance is a positive term of the objective's risk, the builder marks variance_flag on the namespace that owns the weights, mark_objective_variance!. A semidefinite phylogeny on the same weights then adds no p · tr(W) penalty when the objective minimises the risk. A variance that is only a bound does not mark the flag, and the penalty stays. sdp_variance_flag! selects the formulation, and the source of the covariance matrix, the measure or the prior, does not change it. A measure that holds its matrix reads no prior, so a programme Allocation Set that reads no rows passes pr = nothing.

  • i: Constraint index for unique variable and constraint naming.

  • opt::RiskBoundOwner: The owner of the constraint.
  • pr: The prior result, or nothing when r holds its covariance matrix and has no risk-contribution rows.
  • pl: Optional phylogeny constraints.

  • variance_risk: The variance expression.

Add a variance to the model of a FactorRiskContribution, always in the semidefinite formulation over the decision vector.

The asset weights are $\boldsymbol{w} = \mathbf{P} \boldsymbol{z}$. With flag = false the decision vector is the factor weights, $\boldsymbol{z} = \boldsymbol{w}_1$ and $\mathbf{P} = \mathbf{B}_1$, which is formulation 16 of [185]. With flag = true it holds the off-factor weights too, $\boldsymbol{z} = [\boldsymbol{w}_1; \boldsymbol{w}_2]$ and $\mathbf{P} = [\mathbf{B}_1\ \mathbf{B}_2]$. $\mathbf{P}$ is then square and invertible, so the problem is the asset formulation of Variance in the basis of the factors. The model lifts $\boldsymbol{z}$ into $\mathbf{W}_z$, and the variance is the trace of $\mathbf{P}^\intercal \mathbf{\Sigma} \mathbf{P} \mathbf{W}_z$. At rank one it is the variance of the returned weights under either value of flag.

The risk-contribution rows of r.rc read the diagonal of sigma_W_, one entry for each factor. At rank one entry $j$ is $[\boldsymbol{w}_1]_j [\mathbf{B}_1^\intercal \mathbf{\Sigma} \boldsymbol{w}]_j$, the Euler contribution of factor $j$ that factor_risk_contribution reports, and the rows state it as a share of the whole variance. The off-factor weights take no row, and their contribution is the remainder.

When sdp_objective_lift_flag is true, under MaximumRatio with SquareRootRatio, the objective term and the rows read $\mathbf{W}_c$ of set_sdp_frc_objective_lift! in place of $\mathbf{W}_z$, so the term has degree two and the model maximises the Sharpe ratio. The bound reads $\mathbf{W}_z$, and the method builds that trace only when settings.ub holds a bound.

JuMP formulation

Variables

  • frc_W: read, $\mathbf{W}_z$, the lifted matrix that set_sdp_frc_constraints! builds once.
  • lift_frc_W: read under the lift of the objective, $\mathbf{W}_c$.

Expressions

  • sigma_W_: $\mathbf{B}_1^\intercal \mathbf{\Sigma} \mathbf{P} \mathbf{W}_z$, the $N_f$ rows of the factors, registered without the lift of the objective.
  • variance_risk_: $\mathrm{Tr}(\mathbf{P}^\intercal \mathbf{\Sigma} \mathbf{P} \mathbf{W}_z)$, registered without the lift of the objective, and with it when settings.ub holds a bound.
  • lift_sigma_W_, lift_variance_risk_: the same two expressions on $\mathbf{W}_c$, registered under the lift of the objective.

Each name carries the index i of the measure, and the namespace prefix.

Where:

  • $\boldsymbol{z}$: The decision vector, $\boldsymbol{w}_1$ or $[\boldsymbol{w}_1; \boldsymbol{w}_2]$.
  • $\mathbf{W}_z$: Lifted matrix of the decision vector, symmetric, with $\mathbf{W}_z \succeq \boldsymbol{z} \boldsymbol{z}^\intercal / k$.
  • $\mathbf{P}$: The basis of the decision vector, b.
  • $\mathbf{B}_1$: The pseudoinverse of $\mathbf{B}^\intercal$, the first $N_f$ columns of $\mathbf{P}$.
  • $\mathbf{B}_2$: The basis of the off-factor directions.
  • $\boldsymbol{w}_2$: The off-factor weights.
  • $\mathbf{B}$: Loading matrix, of size $N \times N_{f}$.
  • $\boldsymbol{w}_1$: Factor weights of FactorRiskContribution, one entry for each of the $N_f$ factors. The asset weights are $\mathbf{B}_1 \boldsymbol{w}_1$, with $\mathbf{B}_1$ the pseudoinverse of $\mathbf{B}^\intercal$, plus an off-factor block when flag = true.
  • $\mathbf{\Sigma}$: Covariance matrix of the risk measure, $N \times N$. It is the measure's own matrix when the measure states one, and the prior's otherwise.
  • $k$: Variable that scales the budget in the homogenised problem.

Relaxation

The encoding is not exact: the entries below bound the quantity instead of reproducing it, and the bound is tight only under the condition stated here.

  • variance_risk_ lies at or above $\boldsymbol{w}^\intercal \mathbf{\Sigma} \boldsymbol{w} / k$. It is tight at $\mathbf{W}_z = \boldsymbol{z} \boldsymbol{z}^\intercal / k$, and rc_variance_constraints! states the condition for its rows.

Arguments

  • model::JuMP.Model: The JuMP optimisation model.
  • i: Constraint index for unique variable and constraint naming.
  • r::Variance: The measure.
  • opt::FactorRiskContribution: The optimisation estimator.
  • pr: Prior result.
  • b::MatNum: $\mathbf{P}$, the basis that maps the decision vector to the asset weights.

Returns

  • variance_risk: The variance expression.

Add the worst-case variance of an uncertainty set to model, bound it by settings.ub, and add it to the risk that the objective reads.

The set is the measure's own ucs, fitted with sigma_ucs when it is an estimator. The fit reads the optimisation's returns rd and its prior, because an AbstractPriorUncertaintySetEstimator fits from the prior result, and an estimator that carries its own pe drops it. The centre falls back to the measure's sigma and then to the prior's. The bound settings.ub is in the units of a variance, and ucs_variance_risk_bounds! names the expression of degree one that it acts on.

When sdp_lifted_set and sdp_objective_lift_flag are both true, the objective term of the box, the ellipsoid or the norm ball reads the lift with a constant corner of set_sdp_objective_lift!, so it has degree two under MaximumRatio. set_ucs_variance_risk! then builds the term on that lift, with its own names under the namespace lift_, and builds the worst case on the lift of set_sdp_constraints! for the bound only when settings.ub holds one.

  • model::JuMP.Model: The JuMP optimisation model.
  • i: Constraint index for unique variable and constraint naming.

  • opt::RiskConstraintOwner: The owner of the risk constraint, a risk-based JuMP optimisation estimator or a programme Allocation Set.
  • pr: Prior result.

Keyword arguments

  • prefix::Symbol: Model State namespace of the build (default: empty, i.e. the bare key).
  • rd::ReturnsResult: The optimisation's returns, read by an estimator of the set (default: an empty ReturnsResult).
  • kwargs...: Passed to sigma_ucs.
